[plywood](https://intentius.io/plywood/) runs Pulumi stacks through chant's
gates. `plywood estate` writes its estate and audit record to the bucket
`reports.bucket` names in `plywood.yml`, in chant's report-store format.
behold reads that bucket and shows it in the **Reports** tab.

```sh
npx @intentius/behold serve . --terragucci s3://acme-plywood-reports
npx @intentius/behold serve . --terragucci gs://acme-plywood-reports
npx @intentius/behold serve . --terragucci az://acme/plywood-reports
npx @intentius/behold serve . --terragucci ./reports         # a synced copy, or file://
```

## What it reads

Each key with one GET, and never with a list:

| Key | What behold takes from it |
|---|---|
| `estate/estate.json` | `chant.estate/v1`: each stack's counts, the waves waiting at a gate and the newest runs |
| `estate/dora.json` | `chant.dora/v1`: the delivery numbers |
| `audit.jsonl` | `chant.audit/v1` lines for the timeline |

`s3://` is read as for [terragucci](/behold/using/terragucci/#what-it-reads).
`gs://` and `az://` are read through chant's report store with the
credentials in the environment, the ones plywood's jobs write with.

Every document is checked against the JSON Schemas chant publishes at
`@intentius/chant/report-store/<kind>.schema.json`. One that does not match
is refused by name with `code: "terragucci-report"`.

## The tab

The head names the project and says when the newest run finished. The
estate's counts come next, linked to `estate/estate.html`, with the newest
runs under them. Every wave waiting at a gate gets a box naming its stack and
gate. The timeline from `audit.jsonl` closes the tab with the newest entry on
top, and each line says who acted.

An approval is a signed record on the gate ledger, made with
`plywood approve` at your shell. behold does not approve.

Without `--terragucci-project` the tab shows the whole estate, every stack's
waiting waves together. Naming one stack with `--terragucci-project <stack>`
reads that stack alone and adds its delivery numbers.

An agent reads the estate with `GET /api/terragucci`, whose answer carries
`product: "plywood"` beside `waiting` and `recent`. The audit record is at
`GET /api/terragucci/timeline`.
